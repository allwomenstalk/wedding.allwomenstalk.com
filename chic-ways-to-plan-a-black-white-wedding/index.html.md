# 5 Chic Ways to Plan a Black & White Wedding

I saw a stunning bouquet of **anemones** on [Martha Stewart Weddings](https://www.marthastewart.com/7874180/unique-bouquets) and thought what an elegant choice for black and white flowers at a wedding. When I started searching, I realized that black and white themed weddings are a timeless classic, but you can never have enough inspiration when planning your big day. From sleek bridal gowns to black and white wedding bouquets ideas, monochrome styling offers endless sophistication. Here are 5 chic ways you can plan a **black and white wedding**...

## 5. Bride & Groom...

![Bride & Groom...](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2011/07/couple_403x504.jpg)

Photo: [Linda Wallace](https://lindawallacephotography.com/) via [Martha Stewart Weddings](https://www.marthastewart.com/7918336/romney-and-daron-new-york-city)

Are they not the most adorable couple you have ever seen, and just so perfect for my **black and white wedding** post? From her gorgeous gown and his custom black and white [tuxedo look](https://wedding.allwomenstalk.com/4-tuxedo-looks-for-your-royal-prince/) to their matching black umbrellas (giving off total Luv Betsey umbrella vibes) and her unique headpiece by [Oscar de la Renta](https://goo.gl/53Rmpq), they just scream **city chic**. This beautiful bride had the brilliant idea of getting a short dress by [Fendi](https://goo.gl/KMVqgH) knowing they would be running around the city for photos. When you plan wedding dress options, always consider your location and comfort, no matter what vision you initially started with.

Here are a few quick tips when planning monochromatic bridal attire:

- Consider a short reception dress for easier mobility during city photo sessions.
- Pair a tailored tuxedo with crisp white silk accents.
- Coordinate matching accessories like sleek black umbrellas for unexpected weather.

## 4. Bridesmaids...

![Bridesmaids...](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2011/07/bridesmaid_412x481.jpg)

Photo: [Eliza J](http://www.zappos.com?ref=allwomenstalk.com) via [Zappos](https://goo.gl/QCqYQi)

Why settle for choosing either black or white when you can combine both colors into a trendy black and white [bridesmaid dress](https://allwomenstalk.com/7-lovely-alternatives-to-bridesmaids-dresses/) your bridal party will genuinely love wearing? This beautiful **floral print dress** from [Zappos](https://goo.gl/QCqYQi) is one that bridesmaids can easily wear again to bridal showers, baby showers, and summer cocktail parties. Not only is this a striking **color palette** for your ceremony, but it is also a timeless color combination that will never go out of style. Don't forget to complement their dresses with sweet details like [black and white cookie favors](#1) at their places.

## 3. Accessories...

![Accessories...](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2011/07/bracelet_415x312.jpg)

Photo: [Betsey Johnson](http://www.betseyjohnson.com?ref=allwomenstalk.com) via [Zappos](https://goo.gl/wRJKTr)

Don't stop at your bridesmaids and floral arrangements when combining these **chic colors**. Show off your personal aesthetic with [fun bridal accessories](https://wedding.allwomenstalk.com/ways-to-go-bright-bold-with-bridal-jewelry/) like this black and white **charm bracelet** by [Betsey Johnson](http://www.betseyjohnson.com?ref=allwomenstalk.com). The little kisses on the heart charms offer a playful touch to show off your romantic side!

## 2. Black & White Favors...

![Black & White Favors...](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2011/07/blackandwhitecookie_403x504.jpg)

Photo: [MarthaStewartWeddings](https://www.marthastewart.com/7854949/annette-and-dan-new-york-city), Cookie: [WilliamGreenbergDessert](https://web.archive.org/web/http://www.wmgreenbergdesserts.com/), Thumbprint Heart Label:[AnnetteBerryDesign](https://web.archive.org/web/http://www.annetteberrydesign.com/new/#home)

You only get one wedding day and one chance to go all out with your theme. Why not carry your monochrome palette from head to toe, from your [wedding bouquet](https://wedding.allwomenstalk.com/4-sweet-simple-wedding-bouquets/) to bridesmaids, and cake to **cookie favors** in black and white? With these classic tones, you can never have too much elegance. If you are hosting in the NYC area or want to order online, try [William Greenberg's](http://wmgreenbergdesserts.com?ref=allwomenstalk.com) iconic **black and white cookie favors**—renowned as some of the finest in New York! The matching ribbon and custom heart thumbprint label designed by the bride add an unforgettable personalized element.

Here is how classic and modern monochrome wedding elements stack up:

| Category | Classic Choice | Modern Refresh |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attire | Full-length gown & black tux | Short Fendi dress & silk accents |
| Favors | Sugared almonds | Black and white cookie favors |
| Desserts | Plain white frosting | Sugar anemones & candy bar |

## 1. Cake & Candy...

![Cake & Candy...](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2011/07/dessertbar_403x504.jpg)

Photo: [MarthaStewartWeddings](https://www.marthastewart.com/7854949/annette-and-dan-new-york-city), Cake: Fortunato Brothers, Sugar Anemones: [SugarFlowerShop](https://web.archive.org/web/http://www.sugarflowershop.com/)

How amazing are the details of these black and white **sugar anemones** on this wedding cake? It is hard to believe they aren't real flowers! The simplicity of an all-white cake accented with sugar anemones from [SugarFlowerShop](https://web.archive.org/web/http://www.sugarflowershop.com/) creates an eye-catching statement where subtle details make a huge impact. Along with the centerpiece cake (don't forget creative [wedding cake toppers](https://allwomenstalk.com/7-awesome-wedding-cake-toppers/)), the couple featured a matching **black and white candy bar** that guests absolutely adored.
