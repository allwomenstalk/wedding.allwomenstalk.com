# 7 Bachelorette Party Do's: Essential Ideas for an Unforgettable Celebration

When it comes to planning a bachelorette party, there is a long list of things you might want to avoid—like getting totally wasted, doing things you might regret later, posting embarrassing moments online, or ending up with a headache that lasts for days. A bachelorette party should be a celebration the bride loves to remember, not something she wishes she could forget. If you're wondering what to do for a bachelorette party that keeps the energy high and the bride happy, focusing on memorable activities is key.

So, what are the best bachelorette party things to do? Whether you are looking for low-key relaxation or a lively night out, here are seven essential bachelorette party do’s to help you plan an unforgettable celebration for the bride-to-be.

## 1. Have a Nice Meal

![Have a Nice Meal](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fa/kc/h9w00ey054c8f9abf0623315371417_500x333.jpg)

Picking the bride’s favorite restaurant and booking a table via platforms like [OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com) is one of the best ways to kick off a successful bachelorette party. Starting the celebration with a great meal allows everyone in the bridal party to sit down, catch up, and set a positive tone for the rest of the event. If the group wants a more intimate setting or is watching their budget, organizing a catered dinner at home or preparing a home-cooked feast together is a fantastic alternative.

Starting with a satisfying meal is an essential rule of thumb because it ensures everyone stays energized and well-hydrated before moving on to evening festivities. To keep meal planning stress-free, consider these quick tips:

- Confirm all dietary preferences and allergies ahead of time.
- Make restaurant reservations well in advance for larger groups.
- Arrange split-check options with the venue beforehand.

## 2. Naughty Activity

![Naughty Activity](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fb/ob/ftjdjdvt54c8f9acd583e667329695_500x322.jpg)

A little playful fun is often a staple of bachelorette celebrations, but tailoring it to the bride's comfort level is key. Organizing a cheeky activity—such as attending a private burlesque workshop, booking a pole fitness class via [ClassPass](https://classpass.com), or scheduling a dance lesson—can be a hilarious bonding experience for the group.

Always keep the bride’s personality in mind when planning these activities. If she enjoys playful humor, a lighthearted dance session or private entertainer might be a huge hit. However, if she tends to be shy, pushing her into an uncomfortable situation could dampen her mood. Communication is key to ensuring everyone has fun while keeping the experience light and positive.

## 3. Pajama Party

![Pajama Party](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/v5/tk/mp07oshq54c8f9adab327391001801_500x333.jpg)

While some groups prefer hitting the town, others love keeping things cozy and casual. If the bride prefers relaxed gatherings over night clubs, hosting a classic bachelorette pajama party is a cozy, low-stress choice. Gather matching sleepwear, set up a movie screen, and bring out your favorite childhood comfort foods.

A pajama party offers a nostalgic throwback to sleepovers with childhood friends, creating a warm atmosphere to share stories, play truth-or-dare, and enjoy quality downtime together. If your group wants to unwind after a [nice meal](#1), settling in for a movie night in plush robes makes for a memorable evening.

## 4. Tropical Weekend

![Tropical Weekend](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/z0/nh/wkjs59n054c8f9aea11f9298276970_500x311.jpg)

If budget allows, planning a weekend getaway or beach vacation provides a wonderful opportunity for the bride to recharge before the big day. Booking a coastal house rental through [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com), lounging by the pool, and enjoying sunny downtime can be just what she needs to unwind from wedding planning stress.

For groups on a moderate budget, a road trip to a nearby lakehouse or cabin can deliver the same relaxing vacation vibes without excessive travel costs. Here are a few essentials for a smooth getaway:

- Plan a balanced itinerary with both group activities and free downtime.
- Stock up on shared snacks, drinks, and beach games.
- Organize group transportation and carpooling in advance.

Whether you choose a tropical resort or a cozy cabin, pairing a relaxing trip with a relaxed [pajama party night](#3) gives the bride the best of both worlds.

## 5. A Dream Came True

![A Dream Came True](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2011/03/519x338_500x338.jpg)

Surprising the bride with an experience tailored specifically to her passions is a powerful way to make her feel special. Think about what she loves most—whether that is hiring a private chef to host an exclusive cooking class, booking a mixology masterclass, or securing tickets to see her favorite local performer.

As the maid of honor or close friend, you know her unique tastes better than anyone. Put thought into a surprise that aligns with her favorite hobbies or dreams, creating a standout moment that will be remembered long after the wedding weekend is over.

## 6. Vintage Theme

![Vintage Theme](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cd/sc/dknr6iep54c8f9af92330187707396_500x338.jpg)

Theme parties are a fun way to bring creative energy to the celebration. A popular idea is picking a retro or vintage theme—whether that means dressing up in glamorous 1920s attire, rockabilly 1950s outfits, or even hunting for retro dresses at local thrift stores for a hilarious photo session.

Make sure the bride is excited about the costume idea before committing to it. If she loves dressing up and striking funny poses with her friends, a vintage theme will make for fantastic photos and lasting memories throughout the night.

## 7. Party Crashing

![Party Crashing](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2011/03/Fotolia_18780801_Subscription_XL_500x326.jpg)

If the bride prefers celebrating alongside her partner, combining forces for a joint bachelor and bachelorette party—or having the groups meet up late in the evening—is a fantastic option. Starting the night with separate activities and then merging for music, dancing, or drinks creates a lively atmosphere where everyone can celebrate together.

A joint celebration takes off the pressure, allowing both parties to enjoy each other's company while keeping the mood relaxed and high-energy. If you decide to bypass a traditional [naughty activity](#2), bringing both groups together for an evening out offers a fantastic way to end the festivities on a high note.

To help you choose the ideal party style for your bride, here is a quick comparison of popular celebration formats:

| Party Type | Ideal For | Key Vibe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fine Dining & Night Out | Foodies & City Lovers | Elegant & High-Energy |
| Cozy Pajama Night | Low-Key Brides | Nostalgic & Relaxing |
| Joint Celebration | Couples with Shared Friends | Social & Inclusive |

What do you think of these bachelorette party do’s? Whether you prefer cozy pajama nights, glamorous dinners, or joint celebrations with the groom’s party, tailoring the event to the bride's personality is what matters most. How are you planning to celebrate your bride-to-be?
