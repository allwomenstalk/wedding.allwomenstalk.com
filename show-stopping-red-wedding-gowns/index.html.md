# 7 Show\-Stopping Red Wedding Dresses for the Bold Bride

I know what you're thinking: "Is she serious, red wedding gowns?!" But wait until you see real brides rocking red wedding dresses—they are absolutely gorgeous! Whether you are dreaming of a dramatic ball gown or a sleek red wedding reception dress, tinted bridal gowns make an unforgettable statement. Legendary designers like [Vera Wang](https://www.verawang.com) have redefined bridal style, crafting show-stopping red wedding attire ranging from bright crimson to deep berry hues. If you're looking for unique [wedding style guides](https://wedding.allwomenstalk.com/) and inspiration, take a look at these seven incredible red wedding gowns. You might just change your mind and embrace an all red wedding theme!

| Red Shade | Best Season | Style Vibe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bright Crimson | Spring & Summer | Bold & Modern |
| Deep Burgundy | Fall & Winter | Regal & Dramatic |
| Ruby Red | Year-Round | Romantic & Classic |

## 1. One Shoulder Red Wedding Gown

![One Shoulder Red Wedding Gown](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2013/04/1_one-shoulder-red-wedding-gown_460x680.jpg)

The color red symbolizes love, power, and passion among other things. It's a strong color that shows a bride who isn't afraid to be bold, daring, and different. I love the texture and layers of this [one-shoulder silhouette](#6) from Vera Wang's collection. It is unique in color and style with a mix of romantic tulle and delicate lace detailing that sets a high standard for red wedding dresses.

- Asymmetrical one-shoulder design for a striking look
- Intricate tulle and lace layering
- Vibrant crimson tone that exudes confidence

## 2. Red Velvet Wedding Gown

![Red Velvet Wedding Gown](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2013/04/2_red-velvet-wedding-gown_600x900.jpg)

This velvet red wedding dress leaves me completely speechless! Would you believe that this stunning creation is a DIY masterpiece put together by photographer Ciara Richardson? The delicate lace top was dip-dyed and attached to a rich red velvet skirt, creating a perfect contrast against a snowy winter backdrop. If you're searching for unique velvet wedding gowns or winter bridal inspiration, stop by [Bridal Musings](https://bridalmusings.com/34377/magical-winter-proposal-shoot-snow-roses-red-velvet-ciara-richardson/) to view the full magical shoot!

## 3. Oscar De La Renta Red Wedding Gown

![Oscar De La Renta Red Wedding Gown](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2013/04/3_oscar-de-la-renta-red-wedding-gown_600x797.jpg)

Vera Wang isn't the only designer seeing red on the bridal runway; icons like [Oscar de la Renta](https://www.oscardelarenta.com) have embraced the trend with a vibrant array of colors. Besides this gorgeous ruffled Spanish-style red wedding dress, Oscar featured a stunning blue tulle gown in his bridal runway collection. It shows how high-fashion designers are elevating red wedding attire for modern brides.

## 4. Simple Red Wedding Dress

![Simple Red Wedding Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2013/04/4_simple-red-wedding-dress_550x824.jpg)

I love brides who wear a red wedding dress simple yet chic, turning an understated silhouette into something utterly fabulous. This beautiful bride enjoyed a no-muss, no-fuss day from her Town Hall ceremony straight to the restaurant reception. Her scarlet gown from online retailer [Coast](https://www.coastfashion.com) perfectly reflected her easygoing big day and personality. When you find a style that works for you, go with it—no questions asked!

## 5. Vintage Red Wedding Dress

![Vintage Red Wedding Dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2013/04/5_vintage-red-wedding-dress_600x900.jpg)

I'm really jealous of brides who look amazing in vintage wedding gowns! Colored wedding dresses are great for all brides, but they create a fantastic story for those who are eloping, getting married at City Hall, or looking for a stunning red reception dress for bride after-parties. I love the way the sunlight glows on this bride's gown; it's the perfect look for a romantic red vintage wedding in the woods!

## 6. Deep Red Wedding Gown

![Deep Red Wedding Gown](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2013/04/6_deep-red-wedding-gown_600x900.jpg)

The rich tone of this deep red wedding dress is perfect for a Fall or Winter celebration. It is another lovely color that shines against a snow-filled background or autumn scenery. Picture a gorgeous blend of fall leaves, orange pumpkins, and a bride in a show-stopping deep red wedding gown... absolute perfection! After introducing black gowns for Fall, Vera Wang impressed us even more with deep reds, proving that non-traditional bridal gowns are here to stay.

## 7. Princess Red Wedding Gown

![Princess Red Wedding Gown](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wedding/2013/04/7_princess-red-wedding-gown_600x900.jpg)

Yes, another Vera Wang red wedding gown, but I just couldn't resist... can you blame me? This princess-like ball gown is breathtaking, featuring an intricate lace-trimmed neckline and cascading layers. For traditional brides falling in love with this dramatic silhouette, it can also inspire a classic white wedding dress with red trim or subtle red accents. It's truly a fairytale look for anyone dreaming of bold red wedding dresses.
